Forumind helps you catch up on any Discourse forum in seconds. It's a forum browser with an AI assistant beside it: summarize long topics, ask follow-up questions, and let the assistant search the whole forum for you, with links to the posts it used.

SKIP THE SCROLL
Get the main points of a 500-reply topic without reading every post: the original post, what people are saying, and tips from the thread. Later, tap Check for new replies and only the new posts are read.

ASK QUESTIONS
Chat about the topic you're reading: "What did people decide?" or "Is there a workaround?" Edit a question to branch the conversation, and set instructions just for that topic.

ASK THE FORUM
Ask a question and the assistant searches the forum, reads the most relevant topics, and answers with numbered sources you can tap to check.

ANY DISCOURSE FORUM
Open a forum and the app recognizes it. Pin the forums you use most; the ones you visit show up under Recent. If a forum needs you to log in, log in right in the app. Your login is sent only to that forum.

SHARE FROM SAFARI OR CHROME
Share any forum page to Forumind to open it in the app, summarize it, chat about it, or ask the forum.

WATCH TOPICS
Get a notification when a topic you care about has new replies.

YOUR CHOICE OF AI
Use Apple Intelligence where it's available: no account and no key needed. Or bring your own provider: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, xAI, DeepSeek, NVIDIA NIM, or a model running on your own computer with Ollama or LM Studio.

A CLEANER BROWSER
The built-in browser blocks ads and trackers using the EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists. Allow ads on any site with one tap.

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
Built for iPhone and iPad, with the forum and the assistant side by side on iPad. Sync your forums, summaries, chats, and settings through a folder in your own iCloud Drive, encrypted with a key kept in your iCloud Keychain.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- There are no Forumind servers. The app talks directly to the forum and to the AI you choose.
- API keys are stored in your Keychain.
- Your history stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.
