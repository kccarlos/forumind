## Forums belong to their communities

Forumind shows forums that other people and organizations run. Their posts,
accounts and rules are theirs, and each forum's own terms and guidelines also
apply when you read or post there. Forumind has no accounts of its own: when
you log in, you log in to the forum, on the forum's own website.

## Reporting and blocking

- **Report:** on any topic, open the browser's **⋯** menu › **Report or
  block** to report the topic, a post, or a user. The report goes to the
  developer by email. You can also write to
  [kccarlos.opensource@gmail.com](mailto:kccarlos.opensource@gmail.com).
- **Block:** from the same screen, block a user to hide everything they post
  on that forum, right away, in the browser and in summaries, chat, and Ask
  the forum. Blocking also lets the developer know. Manage blocked users in
  **Settings › Data & privacy**.
- **Filter:** in **Settings › Data & privacy**, add words or phrases to hide
  posts that contain them.

## What the developer does with reports

Reports are reviewed **within 24 hours**. Content that breaks these terms is
removed from Forumind for every user, and the user who posted it is blocked
in the app, through the public moderation list the app downloads
([moderation/](https://github.com/kccarlos/forumind/tree/main/moderation)).
Reports are also passed on to the forum's own moderators when appropriate,
since only they can delete content or accounts on the forum itself.

## AI features

Summaries and answers are generated by the AI provider you choose and can be
wrong or incomplete. Check important information in the original posts,
which the app links to.
